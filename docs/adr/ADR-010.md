# ADR-010: Modular monolith with four processes in three planes

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Trevor Kauyu
- **Source:** Architecture & Design v0.1, section 10

## Context

Separation of duties is needed for safety without the cost of many microservices.

## Decision

Modular monolith with four processes in three planes.

## Consequences

Module boundaries are enforced by import-linter; modules can be split into services later.
